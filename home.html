About MAsREE

Connecting research, education and professional learning since 2016.

MAsREE traces its formation to a meeting of senior academicians on 10 March 2016. The association was formed to expand research and education expertise, share scholarly outputs and strengthen cooperation among people engaged in research, management, policy and planning.

Strengthen research activityDisseminate knowledgeFoster professional cooperationBuild public awareness through seminars & workshops

ICOFEA 2026 EduTrip package

Learn. Connect. Then explore Batam, Indonesia.

Extend the ICOFEA experience with a three-day conference-and-edutrip package from 4–6 December 2026. The programme combines travel from Kuala Lumpur, the ICOFEA 2026 conference in Johor Bahru and a post-conference visit to Batam, Indonesia.

Package fee: MYR 1,100 per person. This EduTrip is an optional add-on package with a separate fee from the standard ICOFEA conference registration.

04 Dec 2026 · Friday

Day 1 · Kuala Lumpur → Johor Bahru

Depart from Kuala Lumpur and check in at the hotel.

05 Dec 2026 · Saturday

Day 2 · ICOFEA + Batam transfer

Attend ICOFEA 2026 at Impiana Hotel, Johor Bahru, then transfer to Batam by ferry, dinner and hotel check-in.

06 Dec 2026 · Sunday

Day 3 · Batam City Tour

Batam city tour, lunch and return journey to Kuala Lumpur.

Package includes

  • Tour-bus transport
  • 4-star hotel accommodation in Johor Bahru and Batam
  • Ferry ticket and tax
  • Meals as stated in the itinerary: breakfast, lunch, dinner and tea time
  • Tour and entrance tickets
  • Tour leader from Malaysia
  • Local tour guide in Batam
  • Indonesia Arrival Card

Package excludes

  • Gratuities / tips
  • Porter service
